# Presay User Guide

Presay pre-tests a page, ad, image or message against a panel of synthetic (AI-generated) personas you define, then returns a statistical read and a recommendation: accept it as it is, or revise it and here is what to change.

> Presay is in private preview. The respondents are simulated. Treat results as a directional signal for comparing variants and deciding what to test next, not as a substitute for research with real people. See [Limits of Synthetic Research](/limits.md).

## How a study works

1. **Define the audience and sample size.** Start from a preset or build your own mix. Sample from 1 to 500 personas.
2. **Provide a target.** A URL, image, pasted text, document, audio or video. Every persona sees it identically.
3. **Simulations run.** Each persona reacts in character and scores clarity, appeal, trust and relevance on a 1 to 5 scale.
4. **Read the report.** Per-metric means with 95% bootstrap intervals, a segment breakdown, a narrative synthesis, an accept-or-revise decision, and every individual reaction. Share it as a read-only link or export it.
