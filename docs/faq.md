# FAQ

## What is Presay?

Presay pre-tests a page, ad, image or message against a panel of synthetic personas you define, then returns a statistical read and a decision: per-metric means, bootstrap intervals, segment breakdowns, every individual reaction, and an accept-or-revise call with the passages to change.

## How does a study work?

You define the audience, pick a target (a URL, an image, pasted text, a document, audio or video) and a sample size from 1 to 500 personas. Each persona reacts on a fixed rubric, and Presay aggregates the reactions into a report you can share as a read-only link or export as PDF, Word or JSON. See [Your First Study](/getting-started/first-study.md).

## Can synthetic personas replace real user research?

No. Personas are model-generated, so results are a directional signal for comparing variants and deciding what to test next. See [Limits of Synthetic Research](/limits.md).

## How many personas do I need?

Larger samples give tighter intervals. The form shows the margin of error and confidence tier for your sample size before you run, and segments below n = 5 are suppressed. See [Audience & Sample Size](/studies/audience.md).

## What do I do with the result?

A report on one target leads with an accept-or-revise call and quotes the passages behind each recommendation. For pasted text, **Apply and retest** drafts a revision and scores it against the original. You can also compare two variants in one study. See [Improve & Retest](/drafts/retest.md).

## Can I use Presay from an AI assistant?

Yes. Connect an assistant such as Claude from **Settings, AI agents**. It asks you to sign in with your Presay account and approve access, spends your own study allowance, and you can disconnect it any time. See [Connect an Assistant](/ai/mcp.md).

## Why was a segment hidden?

Segments with fewer than 5 personas are suppressed because their averages are too noisy to read. Run a larger sample, or widen the segment. See [Segments & Reactions](/reports/segments.md).

## Why do I get different numbers when I re-run a study?

Studies are simulations, so some run-to-run variation is expected. To compare versions fairly, run them as one A/B study on the same panel.

## What does "directional signal" mean?

A 0 to 100 summary of how favourably the simulated panel reacted across all four metrics. Use it to compare variants, not as an absolute score or a forecast.

## Is my content private?

Your content is sent to AI models to run the study, and is visible to you and anyone you share a report with. See [Settings & Privacy](/account/settings.md).

## I reached my study limit
