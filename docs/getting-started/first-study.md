# Your First Study

A first study takes a few minutes to set up. This walkthrough tests a headline on a US general-population panel.

## 1. Start a new study

Open **Studies** and choose **New study**. Pick what you want to test:

- **A/B test** to compare two versions
- **Concept test** for an idea, feature or product concept
- **Messaging test** for one headline, description or piece of copy

For this walkthrough choose **Messaging test**. See [Study Types](/studies/types.md) for the differences.

## 2. Add your content

Paste the text into **Target text**, for example ad copy, an email draft or a product description. You can also use a URL, an image, a document, or audio and video. See [Choosing a Target](/studies/targets.md).

Giving the study a name is optional but makes it easy to find later.

## 3. Choose an audience

Under **Demographics**, pick a **Preset** or choose **Custom** to build your own age, gender, geography, income, education and area mix. See [Audience & Sample Size](/studies/audience.md).

## 4. Choose how many personas

Set **Sample size (n)** between 1 and 500. The form shows the worst-case margin of error and the confidence tier for your choice before you run. For a first pass, 20 to 30 personas is a quick read; larger samples are tighter but take longer.

## 5. Run

Choose **Run study**. The form shows an estimate of how long it will take. The study keeps running if you leave the page. It uses one study from your [allowance](/account/limits.md).

## 6. Read the report

When the study completes, open it from **Studies**. Start at the top: the accept-or-revise decision and the directional signal, then the metrics and the narrative. [Reading a Report](/reports/reading.md) walks through every section.

> Results are simulated and directional. Use them to compare options and decide what to test next.
