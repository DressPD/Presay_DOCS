# What is Presay?

Presay is a web product for pre-testing a page, ad, image or message before it goes live. You describe the people you want to hear from and how many of them. Presay builds that many distinct synthetic personas, shows each one your content, and hands back a statistical read.

It is a product of [Hyver](https://hyver.app/), currently in private preview.

## What you get from a study

- **Scores** on four metrics (clarity, appeal, trust, relevance), each on a 1 to 5 scale, with 95% bootstrap intervals.
- **A directional signal** from 0 to 100 summarising how favourably the simulated panel reacted. Use it to compare variants.
- **A segment breakdown** by age, gender and area, with thin segments flagged.
- **A narrative synthesis** that cites the reactions behind each statement.
- **An accept-or-revise decision** with the passages to change.
- **Every individual persona reaction**, tagged with persona ID, segment and scores.

## When to use it

| Moment | How Presay helps |
| --- | --- |
| Before a redesign ships | Test a landing page against the demographic mix you actually acquire from |
| Before ad spend | Screen creative and thumbnails for clarity and appeal across age and geography splits |
| Before a research budget | Get a directional read fast, then decide whether a real user panel is worth commissioning |
| Before sending a message | Paste a headline, value proposition or email subject line and see where the message splits |

## What it is not

Presay is not a general-purpose survey tool and it does not replace research with real people. The personas are generated by a model. Read [Limits of Synthetic Research](/limits.md) before you make a decision on the results.
