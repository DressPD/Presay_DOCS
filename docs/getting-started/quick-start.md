# Quick Start

Go from nothing to a first read in about five minutes.

**You need:** an email address, and one short piece of content to test, such as a headline, an email subject line or a paragraph of ad copy. Nothing to install.

## Five steps

| # | Do this | Time | More detail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Create an account** at [presay.hyver.app](https://presay.hyver.app/) with your email and a password, then enter the code Presay emails you. | 1 min | [Create an Account](/getting-started/account.md) |
| 2 | Choose **Studies**, then **New study**. Pick **Messaging test** and paste your text into **Target text**. | 1 min | [Study Types](/studies/types.md), [Choosing a Target](/studies/targets.md) |
| 3 | Under **Demographics**, choose a **Preset** for your country. Set **Sample size (n)** to **20 to 30**. | 1 min | [Audience & Sample Size](/studies/audience.md) |
| 4 | Choose **Run study**. It keeps running if you leave the page. | about 1 min | [Running & Managing Studies](/studies/running.md) |
| 5 | Open the report from **Studies**. Read the decision first, then the scores and the narrative. | 2 min | [Reading a Report](/reports/reading.md) |

## What you should see

- A **decision** at the top: **Accept: use it as it is**, or **Revise: change it first** with ranked recommendations.
- A **directional signal** from 0 to 100, and four scores (clarity, appeal, trust, relevance) with their intervals.
- A **narrative** that cites the persona reactions behind each statement.

With 20 to 30 personas the intervals are wide. Treat the result as a first impression and a list of things to look at, not a finding.

## Before you rely on a number

- Respondents are simulated. A result is a **directional signal** for comparing options, not measured demand. Read [Limits of Synthetic Research](/limits.md).
- Small samples have wide intervals. The form shows the margin of error before you run. See [Metrics](/reports/metrics.md).
- Every study counts against a weekly and daily [allowance](/account/limits.md), whichever way you start it.

Stuck? See [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting.md) or the [FAQ](/faq.md).
