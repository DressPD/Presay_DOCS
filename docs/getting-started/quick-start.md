# Quick Start

Five steps from nothing to a first read. Each step links to the page with the detail.

| # | Do this | Time | Detail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Create an account** at [presay.hyver.app](https://presay.hyver.app/) with your email and a password | 1 min | [Create an Account](/getting-started/account.md) |
| 2 | **New study**, then pick **Messaging test** and paste a headline or short text | 1 min | [Study Types](/studies/types.md), [Choosing a Target](/studies/targets.md) |
| 3 | **Choose an audience** (a preset is fine) and set the sample size to **20 to 30** | 1 min | [Audience & Sample Size](/studies/audience.md) |
| 4 | **Run study.** It keeps running if you leave the page and usually finishes in a minute or two | 1 to 2 min | [Running & Managing Studies](/studies/running.md) |
| 5 | **Open the report.** Read the decision first, then the metrics and the narrative | 2 min | [Reading a Report](/reports/reading.md) |

## Before you rely on a number

- Respondents are simulated. A result is a **directional signal** for comparing options, not measured demand. Read [Limits of Synthetic Research](/limits.md).
- Small samples have wide intervals. The form shows the margin of error before you run. See [Metrics](/reports/metrics.md).
- Every study counts against a weekly and daily [allowance](/account/limits.md), whichever way you start it.
- Stuck? See the [FAQ](/faq.md), or [Ways to Use Presay](/getting-started/ways-to-use.md) for the three front doors (web, assistant, extension).
