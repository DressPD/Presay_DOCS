# Create an Account

Presay is in private preview. Create an account or sign in from [presay.hyver.app](https://presay.hyver.app/).

## Sign up

1. Choose **Create account** and enter your email and a password.
2. Presay emails you a verification code. Enter it on the **Confirm account** screen.
3. Sign in.

Passwords need at least 10 characters, with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number and a symbol.

If the code does not arrive or has expired, use **Resend code**.

## Sign in

Enter your email and password. If you started setting up a study on the public page before signing in, your preview settings are kept for 30 minutes in that browser tab so you can pick up where you left off.

## Forgot your password?

1. Choose **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page.
2. Enter your email to get a reset code.
3. Enter the code and a new password that meets the rules above.

> An account that was never confirmed after sign-up cannot be reset. Sign up again with the same email, or contact support.

## Interface language

The interface is available in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese. Change it any time in [Settings](/account/settings.md). Studies are written in the language you run them in.
