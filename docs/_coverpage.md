# Presay

> Pre-test it on a synthetic panel

Describe who you want to hear from and how many of them. Presay shows your page, image or message to that many synthetic personas and returns a statistical read.

- 🎯 **Targets** — a URL, image, pasted text, document, audio or video
- 👥 **Audiences** — presets or your own age, gender, geography and income mix, 1 to 500 personas
- 📊 **Reports** — means, bootstrap intervals, segments and every persona's reaction
- ✏️ **Decisions** — accept or revise, with the passages to change, then retest the draft
