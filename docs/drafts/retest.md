# Improve & Retest

After a **Revise** call you can turn the recommendations into a draft and test it.

## Draft an improved version

On a report, open **Draft an improved version**. It turns the High and Medium priority recommendations into a draft you can test next. Choose what to draft:

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Improve my text** | Rewrites your text with the recommendations applied |
| **Write new copy** | Writes new copy from the recommendations |
| **Improve my image** | Edits your image (PNG, JPEG or WebP) |
| **Generate a new image** | Creates a new image from the recommendations |

Each draft lists **What changed**, which recommendations were **Not applied**, and the prompt used for images. You can copy or download it, and earlier drafts stay under **Earlier drafts**.

### Image drafts

Image drafts are made by an external image provider. You must confirm that the image prompt, and your image where relevant, is sent to that provider, which processes it **outside the EU**. Image drafts may not be enabled for every account yet.

## Apply and retest

For pasted text, **Apply and retest** does both steps in one click: it rewrites your text with the recommendations, then tests the rewrite against your original on the same panel. It counts as **one study**.

## Score a draft against the original

Any text draft of a text original, or image draft of an image original, can be scored with **Score against original**. This runs an A/B test of the original against the draft on the same panel and counts as **one study**.

The draft or the original may expire, in which case it can no longer be scored.

## Reading the result

The retest report is a normal [A/B report](/studies/types.md). Look at the paired difference per metric and whether it is marked significant, then read the reactions to see why.

> A draft built from simulated personas is not a validated result. Test it with real people before you rely on it. Near-identical wording changes are especially unreliable; see [Limits](/limits.md).

## Limits

- Drafts have their own daily cap, separate from your study allowance. See [Study Allowance](/account/limits.md).
- One draft can be in progress per study at a time.
- Drafts only work on reports that have reviewed recommendations to apply.
