# Study Allowance

Presay limits how many studies each account can run, so a single account cannot exhaust shared capacity.

## Study allowance

| Limit | Current value |
| --- | --- |
| Per week | 30 studies |
| Per day | 10 studies |
| Drafts per day | 20 |

Presay is free within these limits (see the [FAQ](/faq.md)). They are the current production limits and may change during the preview.

- The allowance is **rolling**. Capacity frees up as your oldest study passes 7 days old (weekly) or 24 hours old (daily).
- It counts studies started from the website, the [browser extension](/ai/extension.md) and [AI assistants](/ai/mcp.md) together.
- An **A/B test** counts as one study. So does **Apply and retest** and **Score against original**.
- Your remaining allowance is shown on **New study** and in **Settings**, with when the next slot frees up.

## Other limits

| What | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Personas per study | 1 to 500 |
| Document text | 30,000 characters |
| Supported UI languages | English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese |

## If you hit a limit

The run button explains which limit you reached and when the next slot opens. Nothing is lost: your settings stay in the form.

For more capacity during the preview, contact [support@hyver.app](mailto:support@hyver.app).
