# Settings & Privacy

Open the account menu and choose **Settings**.

## Account

- **Display name**, used across your workspace.
- **Email**, your sign-in address. It is managed by Amazon Cognito and shown read-only.
- **Study usage**, with what you have used and when the next slot frees up. See [Limits & Usage](/account/limits.md).

## Appearance

- **Colour scheme**: System, Light or Dark.
- **Accent colour**: Orange or Purple.

These are stored in your browser.

## Language

Choose English, German, French, Spanish, Italian or Portuguese. New studies are written in the language you run them in, and the report says which.

## AI agents

Connect or disconnect assistants such as Claude. See [Connect an Assistant](/ai/mcp.md).

## Privacy

- **Cookie preferences**: strictly necessary cookies (sign-in and remembering your choices) are always on. Analytics cookies are optional; accept, reject or customise them in the banner, and change your mind here at any time.
- Read the [privacy policy](https://presay.hyver.app/privacy-policy.html).

### What Presay does with your content

Your target and audience are sent to AI models to simulate the personas and write the report. Do not put in anything you are not allowed to share with a cloud AI service. Uploaded image drafts and generated images are processed by an external image provider **outside the EU** and only after you confirm.

Imported audience files are not kept. Presay stores only the resulting demographic mix.

You can delete any study, with its report and reactions, from the **Studies** list.
