# Accept or Revise

A report on a single target leads with a call:

| Call | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Accept: use it as it is** | The panel reacted well enough that no change stands out. |
| **Revise: change it first** | The panel found problems worth fixing. The report lists them. |

The call is a recommendation from the results, not a rule. It does not know your constraints, so use it alongside your own judgement.

## What sits behind it

For a **Revise** call the report lists recommendations ranked **High**, **Medium** or **Low**. Each one has:

- the **passage** it concerns, quoted from your content
- the **evidence**: the reactions and scores that support it
- the **expected effect** if you make the change
- **watch-outs**, the risks of making it

Recommendations are drafted from the study's results and then checked against them by a second model pass. The report marks ones that were revised on that check.

## Decision notes

The report lets you record your own decision and what would change your mind. This is personal review context only and is **not** sent to the study model.
