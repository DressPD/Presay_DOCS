# Segments & Reactions

## Segment breakdown

The segment table shows every metric by age, gender and area, from the same run.

- **Thin segments are flagged.** A segment with fewer than 5 personas is suppressed and labelled **insufficient sample**. It is not silently dropped.
- **Significance is corrected.** A segment marked significantly higher or lower than the rest of the sample has passed a test that corrects for checking every segment and metric together (FDR-corrected, 95% confidence). A blank cell means the difference cannot be told apart from noise at this sample size.
- **Bigger panels see more.** To find real differences between groups, run a larger sample. See [sample size tiers](/studies/audience.md).

> Simulated respondents are known to be least faithful for some groups, for example conservatives, people with lower education and minorities, and this panel has not been checked per group. Read differences between groups with caution.

## Individual reactions

Under **Individual reactions** you can read every persona's response. Each is tagged with a persona ID, segment and scores.

- **Search** the reaction text.
- **Filter** by gender and geography.
- **Sort** by default order, most positive first, or most negative first.

Use the grid to check the narrative. If a statement says "trust was a concern for younger respondents", filter to that segment and read the reactions yourself.

## Evidence in the narrative

Statements in the narrative carry numbered references. Select one to jump to the reaction that supports it, or go back from a reaction to the statement it supports.

## Study content

**Analysed content** in the methodology section shows exactly what each persona received, which is useful when a reaction mentions something you did not expect.
